## What is already established

- The completed compiler case is a genuine selected-baseline miscompile detected by an added feasible-point observer. Its actual panic uses only the primitive Boolean-mismatch part of injectivity; a new direct Manual oracle detects the same conflict. The optional remaining-child query is unnecessary for that panic. It therefore does not establish richer equality-sort cancellation, unique detector power, an enabled optimization, or speedup. See [the explanation's contribution and limits](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/report.md#contribution-and-limits) and [separate query exercise](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/report.md#separate-encoding-exercise-querying-before-the-bad-merge).
- Unmarked n-ary remaining-child reverse congruence is already implemented and validated in the prior E1 slice. It is not an outstanding engine gap. Marked forward disequality and equal-output cancellation still require injectivity; reverse congruence does not. Unknown remains undefined. See [accepted encoding findings](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/findings/encoding.md#law-and-supported-fragment). Statements that reverse reasoning is still unary in the older planning notes describe their historical starting point and are superseded by those findings.
- The existing CSR/matrix C2 screen found little final NoAlias data and no distinct-address commute demand in its tested domain. This closes that screen, not alias analysis generally. C3/C4/C5/C6 remain untested future leads, and no search-exhaustion result follows. See [candidate status](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/candidate-map.md) and [C2 verdict](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/candidate-map.md#c2-screening-verdict-and-selected-next-mechanism).

## Suggested meaning of a richer joint exercise

For the interview/plan to consider: require one explained dependency chain in a real EggCC check or optimization where a valid injectivity fact and disequality reasoning both contribute to the consumer's outcome. Prefer an essential equality-sort cancellation or multi-field structural invariant over another primitive-payload panic. A standalone query that reports a discrepancy already supplied by an oracle, while the real check succeeds or fails independently, should remain a coverage demonstration rather than satisfy this stronger aim.

Record the exact roles: equal outputs imply equal children **only for a justified injective constructor**; unequal children imply unequal outputs under that same contract; unequal results plus equal siblings imply a remaining-child witness by ordinary congruence. Do not present the last law as requiring injectivity. A convincing comparison should retain the existing/default behavior, shared witnesses without a new consumer, an equivalent direct/manual rule with the same law and schedule, and the combined encoding. If claiming that both mechanisms matter, identify what becomes unknown or which consumer result disappears when either essential bridge is omitted, without changing the domain or seeding extra facts in one arm. This is a proposed acceptance criterion, not a decision already accepted by the user. [Prior law separation](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/planning/encoding.md#laws-separate-congruence-from-injectivity), [fair controls](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/planning/consumers.md#2-active-noalias-storage-versus-consumption-first-encoding-led-screen).

## Soundness exclusions to carry forward

- No blanket injectivity of Expr `Bop`, `Top`, or `Concat`, global contextual `Const` cancellation, or semantic two-field addition wrapper. Arithmetic equivalence, Select erasure, associativity/empty tuples, and vacuous unreachable contexts defeat these shortcuts. Free syntax representation and denotational equality are different contracts.
- Mutable intervals, points-to/pointees records, and their refinements are not immutable free data. Real refinement can legitimately collapse earlier distinctions. Unequal sets can overlap; different records do not imply disjointness. Removing `Resolved` guards or making immutable snapshots is a separate semantic/representation proposal, not a spelling change.
- Raw equality-sort `!=` compares current identities; absence of equality or a missing query witness does not prove disequality. Different Expr functions can agree at an input. Expr disequality is not pointwise inequality and cannot alone justify Eq folding or runtime nonalias.
- Static allocation sites, formal parameter names, different anchors, and unequal overlapping intervals are not distinct live addresses. Memory bridges need actual provenance/lifetime, same domain/context/state as applicable, valid offsets, and aliasing/zero-offset controls. Modular i64 arithmetic does not automatically justify pointer-offset wrapping.
- Context tags alone do not prevent cancellation leaking an unscoped child. Impossible branches need explicit bottom/feasibility treatment. `ExprIsPure` alone does not prove totality, termination, or safe speculative evaluation.

These are recorded exclusions, not new counterexamples: [consumer note, lines 107–122](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/planning/consumers.md#6-immutable-metadata-reserve-explicit-no-go-cases), [scoped branch obligation](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/planning/consumers.md#5-remaining-child-reverse-congruence-valid-gap-compiler-bridge-pending), and [memory domain](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/planning/consumers.md#2-active-noalias-storage-versus-consumption-first-encoding-led-screen).

## Acceptance, schedules, and documented negatives

Existing fragment limits remain relevant: scalar constructor tables, unionable output/focused child, primitive or unionable siblings; no container/custom-function/general proof-query extension is implied. Queries read committed rows and create no derived-pair storage or unions. Cancellation is explicitly scheduled, rule-body consumers require `:naive` and rerunning after hidden witnesses/equalities, and `check-contradiction` closes only private normalization. No general joint closure or generative convergence guarantee follows. Generative certificate propagation needs fail-fast audits interleaved before consumers, rather than an unbounded producer saturation followed by an audit. [Reference limits](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/reference.md#the-read-only-known-disequal-query), [recorded scheduling constraints](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/planning/encoding.md#query-closure-storage-and-schedules).

Retain meaningful positive and negative checks, not parser failures: same constructor/position and all required equal siblings; ambiguity, unequal siblings, absent forward/cancellation on unmarked constructors; native primitive versus equality-sort handling; late unions/assertions and explicitly scheduled cancellation; final forbidden union/contradiction; cycles/subsumed rows/lifecycle/no query growth; NEE/EE and relevant scheduler parity. The prior finite oracle validates its bounded fixed-equality fragment, not arbitrary joint closure. [Accepted test scope](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/findings/encoding.md#verification), [planned matrix and limits](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/planning/encoding.md#automated-acceptance-matrix-proposed-for-execution).

For a real compiler claim, preserve source/result semantics and the actual native consumer outcome; reduced replay alone is not full-host impact. Compare the strongest equivalent existing/manual/direct rules, not an artificially expensive materialized-pair baseline. A scoped negative can close a candidate for unsoundness, unreachable domain, existing-rule redundancy, missing consumer, or no incremental outcome. Record the exact tested domain and surviving leads; do not turn that result into global impossibility or exhaustive search. Old descriptive timing observations establish no new performance baseline. [Prior acceptance/stop terms](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/planning/consumers.md#proposed-first-vertical-slice-and-stop-rules), [candidate boundaries](../../eggcc-encoding-impact/candidate-map.md).
