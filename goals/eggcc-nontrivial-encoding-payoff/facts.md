# Accepted facts

- Successful completion requires at least one new EggCC compiler bug that pre-exists the encoding integration in a fresh pinned baseline, or a correctness-preserving reproducible end-to-end compilation or generated-runtime speedup. An unsuccessful search, new analysis, cleaner encoding, changed IR, or unmeasured optimization is not success.
- A qualifying result must materially exceed a direct primitive conflict by demonstrating at least one of these: a causally consumed equality or disequality over equality-sort children, a generic chain through more than one semantic constructor, or injectivity and disequality both being essential. Prefer candidates that demonstrate all three.
- Disqualify any result that the same graph and seeds can reproduce with a constructor-specific check that only compares primitive fields and directly emits the final panic, guard, or fact. The completed ObservedBool true-versus-false findings are excluded by this rule.
- Use a faithful hand-written implementation as a fair comparison. Equivalence to a larger multi-rule manual implementation does not disqualify generic reasoning, but no unique logical power may be claimed; equivalence to the simple primitive-only shortcut does disqualify it.
- For each claimed mechanism, retain the intermediate equality or disequality and demonstrate fixed-input knockout, intermediate-fact rescue, downstream-consumer knockout, manual-comparator, and negative controls while preserving the original graph, seeds, baseline behavior, and input.
- Do not stop at the first qualifying example. Continue seeking stronger and additional examples, prioritizing candidates that combine both encodings, cross multiple constructors, reach a substantial real EggCC consumer, or produce the clearest bug or speedup.
- After each candidate family is resolved, run both a fresh Codex source-analysis pass and a read-only Claude Fable subprocess to generate and rank further hypotheses from the new evidence. Treat model output as proposals and verify every source, API, law, producer, consumer, and claimed payoff independently.
- Each hypothesis starts with a valid-trigger, sound-law, actual-producer, actual-consumer, novelty, and primitive-null eligibility screen, then uses the cheapest falsifier before implementation. If eligible it gets one smallest actual-host/native probe and at most one revision justified by a concrete falsifier; two no-progress cycles close the lead.
- Stop only after the retained Codex and Claude Fable passes produce no new source-backed eligible lead, or every remaining lead requires a substantial EggCC redesign beyond a bounded consumer, analysis, or encoding integration. Record the exhausted lead ledger and mark the goal failed if no qualifying payoff exists; do not claim global impossibility.
- Begin from the strongest retained families: scoped fixed-shift equality/disequality propagation into false-Eq or If elimination, same-mask Bitand reverse disequality followed by marked propagation, fixed-split tuple cancellation beyond projection closure, and a fresh live-region NoAlias screen with an actually blocked hot consumer. Add further families only through the recorded ideation loop.
- Do not count the completed Mul/Add/Sub primitive Boolean observations, structural type checker, LocalCell study, tested CSR/matrix NoAlias domain, inherited snapshots or lints, E1-only branch regression, planted faults, malformed unsupported inputs, or integration-introduced defects as the required new payoff.
- A qualifying bug uses a valid minimized input or independently checkable compiler invariant, reproduces on the fresh pre-integration baseline, has an independent semantic oracle, a localized cause, actual compiler/native coverage, nearby passing controls, and evidence that the encoding materially enabled its discovery or diagnosis.
- A qualifying speedup uses matched valid workloads and configurations, correct outputs, the same native backend, explicit end-to-end timing boundaries, repeated per-workload measurements, a fair baseline and manual comparison, and independent confirmation beyond observed variability. Rule-only timing or failed-versus-successful runs do not qualify.
- Justify every injectivity and disequality law for its actual types, contexts, scopes, effects, feasibility, Bottom behavior, lifetimes, and mutation model. Unsupported, contradictory, infeasible, missing-evidence, timeout, type, and backend outcomes remain explicit and cannot be promoted to facts.
- Allow bounded compiler representations, scoped analysis, consumer bridges, and encoding improvements required by a concrete eligible candidate. Treat a wholesale IR, alias, effect, or path-sensitive redesign as a stop condition unless the user explicitly starts a new goal for it.
- Implemented candidates have registered nonzero tests, positive and negative boundaries, fresh exact source/input/dependency/patch/binary/command/configuration identities, actual emitted and native checks where applicable, and the repository's required full validation without accepting unrelated snapshots or waiving new failures.
- Preserve prior studies, frozen evidence, dirty author work, and unrelated source. Use isolated worktrees and durable ignored evidence, keep memory-pressure blocking disabled, retain finite timeouts and real errors, and do not commit, push, open a PR, add proof support, edit the paper, or use destructive cleanup without separate authorization.
- Deliver a collaborator-readable explanation, compact encoding/consumer reference, separate reproduction guide, machine-readable acceptance map, and complete lead ledger. Clearly separate verified facts, hypotheses, failed leads, the simple-rule exclusion, measured results, and any negative final outcome.
