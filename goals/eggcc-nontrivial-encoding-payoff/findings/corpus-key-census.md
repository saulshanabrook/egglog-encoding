# Existing-corpus key census: producers occur, but no qualifying endpoint surfaced

**This bounded census found no exact existing-program trigger for the QR7, Pack32, or low/high-32 decomposition cases.** Quotient/remainder arithmetic occurs in real benchmarks, and Adler-32 contains a related 16-bit packing step. None of the reviewed cases establishes the complete key relation and then reaches an additional equality/address-reuse consumer. The [synthetic QR7 screen](../../../benchmarks/local/eggcc-nontrivial-encoding-payoff/qr7-screen/report.md) remains a separate source-derived microcase, with a manual phase-ordering opportunity rather than demonstrated corpus applicability.

## Scope and counts

The source is clean U0 `a0bbec127f83249074a46af3fd6f6126c90fe0fb`. The census covers all **199 Bril files** under `benchmarks/passing` (83) and `tests/passing` (116), plus a lexical word-operation supplement over the 25 Rust files in those trees. Slow/failing trees, generated graphs, historical studies, and new synthetic workloads are outside the corpus count.

The retained source inventory records each file's hash, functions, blocks, instructions and operand-use receipts. It finds 76 integer `div` instructions in 36 files, 30 local `x-(x/d)*d` forms, and 16 helper bodies that return such a remainder. Its 47 quotient/remainder co-occurrences in 26 files include the helper's own temporary quotient and duplicate uses; these are **syntactic inventory counts**, not 47 semantic full-key opportunities. All 91 source integer `eq` statements were also inspected. The simple same-divisor decomposition recognizer finds no comparison between two quotients or two remainders that could form the required paired-key guard.

No passing Bril file contains an actual `shl` or `shr` instruction, a literal 32-bit split/mask/radix constant (`32`, `4294967295`, `4294967296`), or an identified complementary high/low-32 construction. The only explicit integer bitwise operation in Bril is the existing lowbit test's `bitand`. Arithmetic functions named `LEFTSHIFT`/`RIGHTSHIFT` are present, but are discussed below rather than inferred to be Pack32. The Rust supplement contains no explicit shifts or 32-bit mask/radix hits under its recorded lexical search.

For every near match, **whether U0 or LLVM removes some other arithmetic work remains unmeasured**. A missing applicability trigger is not backend-removal evidence. No exact valid trigger reached the requested endpoint, so this census launched no compiler, native, or timing experiment. It does not borrow the synthetic QR7 pilot as performance evidence for any of these programs.

## Evidence and limits

[census.json](../../../benchmarks/local/eggcc-nontrivial-encoding-payoff/corpus-key-census/census.json.gz) retains the complete source inventory and hashes; [manual-review.json](../../../benchmarks/local/eggcc-nontrivial-encoding-payoff/corpus-key-census/manual-review.json) retains 14 reviewed groups with exact source excerpts, scope, producer, consumer, missing premise, and explicit untested backend status. Search argv/cwd/output hashes are in `search-receipts.json` and `searches/` beside them.

The final lightweight parser has zero unparsed source statements in this corpus. Independent raw-regex counts agree for `div`, `eq`, shifts and `bitand`. [Sensitivity controls](../../../benchmarks/local/eggcc-nontrivial-encoding-payoff/corpus-key-census/sensitivity-checks.json), excluded from the corpus, recognize the synthetic QR7 positive, reject its mixed-divisor pairing, and detect an explicit word-split fixture. Initial parser versions and outputs are preserved; their missing no-argument/multiline headers were corrected before drawing these counts.

This is not SSA or a path/reaching-definition proof. It summarizes simple remainder helpers and then relies on direct source review for the relevant cases. It does not exclude a different lossless-key law, another repository, a substantially different consumer, or an equality that appears only after lowering/optimization. Reopen this applicability lead on an exact source or graph witness with a sound domain and actual downstream demand. No qualifying corpus bug or speedup was established, and no compiler/engine source was changed.
