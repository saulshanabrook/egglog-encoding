# Demonstrate an EggCC payoff from injectivity or disequality

Find a genuine new EggCC compiler bug using injectivity or disequality, or demonstrate a reproducible measured speedup in generated-program execution or end-to-end compilation. Prefer a case using both encodings, but either may qualify when it materially contributes to the result.

The [accepted facts](facts.md) are the shared understanding. Follow the [approved execution plan](plan.md), including its source-grounded discovery notes, independent correctness checks, baseline comparisons and measurement gates.

**Done condition:** demonstrate either a valid minimized bug that pre-exists the new integration in the pinned EggCC baseline, with independent semantic evidence, localized cause, encoding contribution and passing controls; or a correctness-preserving speedup confirmed beyond observed variability on matched workloads with fair baseline/manual comparisons. Deliver the connected explanation, compact reference, reproduction guide and fresh evidence required by the plan.

An equivalent integration, an unmeasured optimization, a repeated old finding or an unsuccessful search does not satisfy this goal. If explored leads do not yield a qualifying result, retain their falsifiers, report the goal as unmet and reassess the search with the user; do not mark it complete.
